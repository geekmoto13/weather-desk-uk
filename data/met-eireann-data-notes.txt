Observation collection / parameter definitions:
https://opendata2.met.ie/edr/collections/observations-swob-nrt-60min
https://opendata2.met.ie/obs_public/
Temperature C; rain mm in preceding hour; wind/gust knots; pressure hPa at
station elevation. Missing sensor sentinel -99 is excluded. Raw/provisional data.

Daily source catalogue, station coordinates, URLs and field coverage:
ireland-climate-stations.json. Source files accessed 25 October 2026.
Daily intervals are 00:00-24:00 UTC, explicitly required by the CSV adapter.
Temperature indicators 0/1 (positive/negative) accepted; 2/3/4 excluded.
Rain indicator 0 accepted; indicator 2 accepted only for explicit zero (trace).
Positive sums, deposition, estimates and missing observations are excluded.
Key: https://opendata2.met.ie/opendata2/docs/KeyDaily.txt
References need 24/30 baseline years, or 7/8 leap years. Missing references
stay unavailable. Original supplied .csv data is refreshed from its own URL.
The .dly cache format does not make these observations NOAA data.

Forecast: https://prodapi.met.ie/weather/details/LATITUDE/LONGITUDE

Warnings: https://www.met.ie/Open_Data/json/warning_IRELAND.json
All current/future Republic of Ireland land warnings are shown, preserving
headlines and visible text, with timestamps and nationwide scope.

Displayed as time series; missing intervals remain gaps.
